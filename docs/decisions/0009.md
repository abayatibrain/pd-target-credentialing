# ADR-0009: Confidence-score calibration via anchor genes
Date: 2026-05-18
Status: Accepted (Armin 2026-05-18)
Supersedes (conditionally): ADR-0008 if calibration fails.

## Context

ADR-0008 commits to a composite score formula with specific weights. The
weights are defensible in principle but their *specific values* are
calibratable, not derived. Without an explicit calibration check, the
score is just an opinion in a number-shaped wrapper. With a calibration
check, we can say: "given the formula and weights in ADR-0008, here is
how a panel of well-known PD genes ranks vs a panel of housekeeping
genes." That is the kind of evidence a hiring panel can verify in
fifteen seconds from the README.

If calibration fails — if known PD genes do not rank substantially above
housekeeping genes — the score is wrong and ADR-0008 must be superseded
with revised weights. This ADR makes that conditional explicit.

## Options considered

### A. No calibration (trust the formula)
- Pros: simpler.
- Cons: indefensible. Any reviewer will ask "what's the score for SNCA?
  what's the score for ACTB? what's the gap?" If we cannot answer with
  numbers, the score is theater.

### B. Calibration against PD-known-positives only
Compute scores for SNCA, GBA1, LRRK2, PRKN, PINK1. Require all five to
score above some threshold.
- Pros: simple to state.
- Cons: no negative control. A score function that returns 1.0 for every
  gene would pass this check.

### C. Calibration against positives AND negatives, requiring separation
Compute scores for a panel of known PD genes (positives) and a panel of
housekeeping / unrelated genes (negatives). Require a quantified
separation between the two distributions before declaring the score
useful.

## Criteria

1. **Both directions must hold.** Known positives ranking high *and*
   known negatives ranking low are both required. Either alone is
   insufficient.
2. **Quantified, not eyeball.** "Looks like the positives are higher" is
   not a calibration result. An effect-size statistic with a CI is.
3. **Pre-registered threshold.** The pass/fail boundary must be committed
   to *before* the calibration is run, so we cannot move the goalposts.

## Decision

**Option C.** Calibration is a pre-registered check defined as follows:

### Anchor sets (Armin-ratified 2026-05-18)

- **Known positives (PD anchors):** SNCA, GBA1, LRRK2, PRKN, PINK1,
  **VPS35, PARK7 (DJ-1)**. All seven are PD-associated by both human
  genetics and literature with strong consensus. VPS35 and PARK7 added
  per Armin sign-off (Q9.1) to broaden the positive distribution and
  exercise the retromer-trafficking and oxidative-stress mechanism
  branches of the PD genetic landscape (§3.1 of the brief).
- **Known negatives (housekeeping anchors):** ACTB, GAPDH, HPRT1, RPL13A,
  UBC. Housekeeping genes with no PD-specific biology and broad
  expression across cell types.

Note: `DJ-1` is the legacy gene name; the HGNC approved symbol is
**`PARK7`**. The HGNC resolver in `src/pd_target_credentialing/io/hgnc.py`
silently substitutes `DJ-1 → PARK7` with a `WARNING` log entry (per §3.4
of the brief and ADR-0010).

### Calibration procedure

1. Compute the composite score `S(g, "Parkinson's disease")` for every
   anchor gene in both sets, using the pipeline's full result for the
   primary dataset.
2. Compute Cohen's d between the positive-set scores and the
   negative-set scores. Bootstrap (n = 1000 over the gene sets) for a
   95% CI on d.
3. Compute the rank-based separation: minimum rank of any positive
   anchor vs maximum rank of any negative anchor. A passing result has
   no overlap (every positive anchor outranks every negative anchor).

### Pass/fail criteria (pre-registered)

- **Pass:** Cohen's d ≥ 1.0 with 95% CI lower bound ≥ 0.5, *and* zero
  rank overlap between positive and negative anchors.
- **Marginal:** d in [0.5, 1.0). Score is reported but flagged as
  weakly calibrated in the README.
- **Fail:** d < 0.5 *or* any positive anchor ranks below any negative
  anchor. ADR-0008's weights are superseded; this ADR is updated to
  document what was found, and a new ADR proposes revised weights.

### Reporting

A calibration figure (anchor scores with their distributions) is committed
to `results/calibration_anchor_genes.png` for every release. The README
references this figure directly and states the achieved Cohen's d with CI.

## Consequences

- The calibration check is part of the demo notebook; running the demo
  produces the figure.
- A failing calibration **halts the v1.0.0 tag** until ADR-0008 is
  revised and re-tested. This is the right behavior — releasing a
  miscalibrated score is worse than releasing nothing.
- ACTB, GAPDH, HPRT1, etc. are not guaranteed to be perfectly silent on
  PD evidence in OpenTargets (literature spillover happens). If their
  raw OT scores are non-trivial, the calibration check may still pass
  via the DE-bonus term remaining at zero for them — but the README
  must note this.
