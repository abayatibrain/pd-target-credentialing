# ADR-0001: Choice of primary substantia nigra dataset
Date: 2026-05-18
Status: Proposed — awaiting Armin sign-off (§1.3 of the brief)

## Context

Parkinson's disease pathology localizes to the dopaminergic neurons of the
substantia nigra pars compacta. Therapeutic-target credentialing has to
measure expression and disease association in *that* cell population, in
*that* brain region — not in a peripheral surrogate, and not pooled across
regions. The first foundational choice for this repo is therefore: which
public single-nucleus PD dataset will primary results be drawn from?

Three datasets meet the basic shape requirement (human, PD vs control,
single-nucleus, midbrain-or-adjacent). They differ in cohort size, anatomic
specificity, and the cell-type resolution at which they were originally
annotated.

## Options considered

### Option A — Smajić et al. 2022 (*Brain*)
Substantia-nigra-specific single-nucleus RNA-seq. Paper-reported cohort is
on the order of five PD subjects and six controls (exact counts deferred to
the loader, which reads from the dataset's published metadata). Cell-type
annotation in the paper covers neurons, oligodendrocytes, astrocytes,
microglia, OPCs, endothelial, and pericytes, with dopaminergic neurons
labeled as a subset of the neuron compartment.
- **Pros:** anatomic specificity matches the biological question; PD vs
  control reasonably balanced; broad cell-type coverage in the published
  annotation.
- **Cons:** small subject-level N (the dataset is powered for cell-type-level
  DE, not for inference about which *individuals* have a given expression
  state); single cohort, single tissue source.

### Option B — Kamath et al. 2022 (*Nat Neurosci*)
Human midbrain dopaminergic-neuron atlas. Designed to resolve DA subtypes
(e.g., SOX6-positive vulnerable subtypes vs CALB1-positive less-vulnerable
subtypes). Includes PD and control subjects at a larger total subject count
than Smajić.
- **Pros:** the most granular published view of DA subtypes available;
  ideal for cross-validation of DA-neuron-specific findings.
- **Cons:** "midbrain" is broader than substantia nigra alone, so the
  non-DA cell-type composition is not directly comparable to Smajić's
  nigra-restricted sample. The dataset's annotation is also DA-centric;
  glial cell types are less richly resolved.

### Option C — Wang et al. 2024 (*Nat Neurosci*)
PD cortex single-nucleus atlas.
- **Pros:** large sample size; well-annotated.
- **Cons:** cortex, not nigra. Cortical involvement in PD is real but
  occurs at later disease stages and reflects different cellular biology.
  Treating cortical data as a proxy for nigra would conflate two distinct
  questions.

## Criteria for choosing

1. **Anatomic match.** The substantia nigra is the disease site; the
   primary dataset must measure there.
2. **DA-neuron resolvability.** Whatever the primary dataset is, the
   pipeline must be able to identify and analyze dopaminergic neurons
   specifically.
3. **PD vs control balance.** Cohort imbalance compromises every
   downstream statistical test.
4. **Available covariates.** Age, sex, post-mortem interval (PMI), and
   technical batch must all be present in the metadata for the covariate
   model in ADR-0006 to be implementable.
5. **License and reproducibility.** Data must be publicly accessible without
   a DUA, and the version must be pinnable.

## Decision

**Primary: Option A (Smajić 2022).** It is the only one of the three that
directly addresses the anatomic match criterion.

**Cross-cohort validation: Option B (Kamath 2022).** For any finding that
is DA-neuron-specific, the same direction of effect must reproduce in the
Kamath atlas before the finding is reported as DA-anchored in a dossier.
This guards against single-cohort artifacts on the cell type most central
to PD biology.

**Deferred: Option C (Wang 2024).** Out of scope for `v1.0.0`. May return
as a future cortical-extension repo or as a non-DA cell-type comparison
within this repo.

## Consequences

- **Statistical posture.** Subject-level inference will be underpowered.
  This must be stated in the README as a limitation, not as a minor footnote.
- **Pipeline structure.** Every analysis stage from QC onward must be
  cell-type-aware. Bulk DE is not an option (see ADR-0006).
- **Covariate modeling.** Age, sex, PMI, and batch must be modeled
  independently for each cohort before any cross-cohort comparison is made.
- **Integration ADR (ADR-0004) is shaped by this choice.** With ~11 donors
  in Smajić, scVI-class methods are overkill; Harmony-class methods are
  appropriate. ADR-0004 will defend that conclusion separately.
- **Cell-type annotation (ADR-0005) is constrained by this choice.** Kamath's
  DA-subtype labels become the reference for our DA-neuron sub-annotation.
