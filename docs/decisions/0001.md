# ADR-0001: Choice of primary substantia nigra dataset
Date: 2026-05-18
Status: Proposed — awaiting Armin sign-off (§1.3 of the brief)

## Context
Repo 1 credentialing requires PD-vs-control transcriptional measurements
in the substantia nigra at single-cell resolution. Multiple public
datasets could serve. The choice affects which cell types are resolvable,
how powered subject-level inference is, and how well the pipeline
generalizes beyond the chosen dataset.

## Options considered
- **Option A: Smajić et al. 2022 (Brain).** ~6 PD vs ~5 control, ~41k
  nuclei from substantia nigra.
  - Pros: substantia nigra specifically; PD vs control balanced; mid-size.
  - Cons: small N (powered for cell-type DE, not subject-level claims);
    single cohort.
- **Option B: Kamath et al. 2022 (Nat Neurosci).** Human midbrain DA
  neuron atlas, PD vs control, detailed DA subtype labels.
  - Pros: rich subtype labels; DA-centric.
  - Cons: midbrain (broader than nigra alone); subtype focus may obscure
    non-neuronal context.
- **Option C: Wang et al. 2024 (Nat Neurosci).** PD cortex single-nucleus
  atlas.
  - Pros: large; well-annotated.
  - Cons: cortex, not nigra — different biology; weaker match to the
    question.

## Criteria for choosing
- Brain region match to the question (substantia nigra is the disease site).
- Cell-type resolution adequate for DA-neuron-specific claims.
- PD vs control balance and per-subject sample size.
- Documented metadata for batch / age / sex / PMI covariate modeling.

## Decision
**Primary: Smajić 2022 (Option A).** It is the most direct
substantia-nigra-specific match to the biological question. Secondary
validation against **Kamath 2022 (Option B)** for DA-neuron-specific
findings — this gives us cross-cohort replication on the most important
cell type without giving up the nigra-specific framing.

Wang 2024 is dropped from v1.0.0 because the brain region does not match
the question; it can return in a future extension on cortical involvement.

## Consequences
- Subject-level inference will be underpowered. The README must say so.
- The pipeline must be cell-type-aware end-to-end; bulk DE is not an option.
- Covariate modeling (age, sex, PMI, batch) must be implemented for both
  cohorts independently before joint comparison.

## Open questions for Armin
- Confirm the choice of Smajić as primary, or override.
- Confirm that Wang 2024 is OK to defer past v1.0.0.
