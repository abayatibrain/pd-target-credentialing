# ADR-0002: Per-nucleus QC thresholds
Date: 2026-05-18
Status: Accepted (Armin 2026-05-18)

## Context

Single-nucleus RNA-seq (snRNA-seq) data require per-nucleus quality control
before any downstream analysis. Three filters are universally applied:
minimum genes detected per nucleus, maximum mitochondrial transcript
percentage, and doublet flagging. Each threshold has a defensible range
but the *specific* number used must be picked once and committed to,
because the value shapes every subsequent step.

A naive copy-paste of single-cell (whole-cell) defaults will reject too
much of the data: snRNA-seq counts are lower per cell than scRNA-seq
counts (nuclei contain less RNA than whole cells), and nuclei have far
fewer mitochondrial transcripts than whole cells because mitochondria
are mostly in the cytoplasm. This ADR commits to nuclei-appropriate values.

## Options considered

### Min genes per nucleus
- **A1:** 200 (whole-cell default).
- **A2:** 500 (snRNA-seq common practice; matches Smajić 2022's reported
  filter).
- **A3:** 1000 (stricter; loses low-output cell types such as some DA
  subtypes).

### Max mitochondrial %
- **B1:** 20% (whole-cell default).
- **B2:** 10% (intermediate, sometimes used for "lenient" snRNA-seq).
- **B3:** 5% (nuclei-appropriate; reflects that mito transcripts in nuclei
  prep should be a small fraction).

### Doublet detection method
- **C1:** Scrublet.
- **C2:** DoubletFinder.
- **C3:** scDblFinder.
- **C4:** No automated doublet detection (rely only on per-cluster outlier
  inspection).

## Criteria for choosing

1. **Match to nuclei biology** — thresholds must reflect what nuclei
   should actually look like, not what whole cells look like.
2. **Recoverability of rare neuronal subtypes** — DA neurons are a small
   fraction of the nigra, and over-strict filtering will lose them.
3. **Consistency with the primary dataset's published QC** — if Smajić
   2022 used a specific threshold, departing from it without reason adds
   confounding when comparing our results to theirs.
4. **Tooling maturity** — the doublet method must have a stable Python
   API and be tested against nuclei (not just whole cells).

## Decision

- **Min genes per nucleus:** 500 (A2).
- **Max mitochondrial %:** 5% (B3).
- **Doublet detection:** Scrublet (C1), per-sample, with a doublet score
  threshold tuned to the published rate (typically 6–8% expected doublets
  in 10x runs at our loading densities).

A nucleus that fails any of the three filters is excluded. The QC report
records per-sample retention rates; if any sample retains less than 50% of
loaded nuclei, the sample is flagged for manual review rather than
silently dropped.

## Consequences

- Some low-output DA subtypes may be lost at the 500-gene threshold.
  ADR-0005 (cell-type annotation) must be aware of this when validating
  against Kamath's subtype labels: a missing subtype here may reflect QC,
  not biology.
- The 5% mitochondrial threshold is stricter than whole-cell conventions.
  README's Method section must say so explicitly so a reviewer doesn't
  compare it to scRNA-seq norms.
- Scrublet is an additional dependency. Listed in `pyproject.toml`.
